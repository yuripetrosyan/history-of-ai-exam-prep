# History of AI — Exam Prep

Interactive study platform for the VU Amsterdam Bachelor AI **History of AI** course (formerly History of Science). Built around the four lectures and the assignment essay format.

**Live site:** _add the GitHub Pages URL here once Pages is enabled._

## What's inside

- **Essentials** — the bare minimum for each lecture: 5 anchors, 3 dates, 3 frameworks, 2 reusable arguments, 5 drop-in sentences.
- **Lectures** — tier-grouped likely exam questions (Tier 1 = direct from assignment, through Tier 4 = reflective bonus) with model essays in the prof's preferred structure.
- **Writing Toolkit** — opening hooks, transitions, conclusion templates, the prof's body-decomposition method, and an anchor-facts cheat sheet.
- **Study mode** — toggle to hide answers and practise writing your own version first.
